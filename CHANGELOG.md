# Changelog

## [1.4.0] - 07-14-2026

### Added

- **New Biomes**:

    - **Alps**: Tall, snow-capped mountains with mossy cliffs
    - **Fjords**: Deep, narrow river/ocean inlets with steep cliffs
    - **Fungi Forest**: A jungle covered in giant mushrooms
    - **Dune Sea**: Vast desert with rolling sand dunes and occasional oasis

### Fixed

- **Crash**: Fixed a crash that occurred when using the mod with certain versions of TerraBlender 

## [1.3.3] - 05-07-2026

### Fixed

- **Azalea Trees**: Leaves now properly spawn at the ends of branches instead of at branch splits
- **Small Azalea Trees**: Redesigned to have natural curved trunks with branches, matching vanilla azalea appearance
- **Tree Foliage**: All trees now have denser, fuller leaf clusters for a more lush appearance
